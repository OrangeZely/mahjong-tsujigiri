## description

Sharpen your riichi mahjong skills, one tile at a time.

Mahjong Tsujigiri! turns discard decisions and fu calculation into short practice sessions. Each round lasts 60 seconds: choose a discard or answer a four-choice fu question, then review what you missed.

THREE WAYS TO PRACTICE
• Full Flush: advanced discard puzzles using a single suit.
• What to Discard: build efficient hands with no separate question timer inside the 60-second round.
• Fu Practice: read a completed hand and calculate its fu, with a detailed breakdown to review afterward.

ONI CHALLENGE
Add a five-second limit to each question in any mode. Consecutive correct answers double the points, up to 8×. Keep your streak alive!

KEEP IMPROVING
• Review accepted discards, explanations, and fu breakdowns.
• Track your history and best score on your device.
• Climb samurai-inspired ranks and compare scores on all-time, weekly, and mode leaderboards.
• Learn from guides on tile efficiency, full-flush waits, fu, and terminology.
• Switch between Japanese and English in the app.
